Course Title: Digital Signal Processing

Course Code: ETEG 305

Credit Hours: 3

Course Description:

This course intends to intreduce digital signal processing techniques and applications.

Course Contents:

Unit 1: Introduction of Digital Signal Processing

Review of digital signals and systems, Introduction of Digital Signal Processing (DSP).
Overview of DSP in real world application and Importance of mathematical model for DSP.
Introduction of commonty used hardware and software for DSP

Unit 2: Z-Transform

Definition of the z-transform. z-transform Propertics: linearity, Shifting, convolution. Scaling.
Multiplication by K Vector sequences: Relation between the Z-transform and the Fourjer
transform of a sequence. Inverse z-transform: Direct division. Partial fraction expansions. the
inverse integral; System response. Transfer function H(z), Response to sinusoidal input - pale-
zero relationships. Stability test of the discrete lime system.

Unit 3: The Discrete Fourier Transform and Signal Spectrum

The Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) derivation, Praperties of the DFT, DFT of non-periodic
digital signals, Signal spectrum. Spectral Estimation using window functions.

Unit 4: Introduction of the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)

FFT computation algorithms: Redix- 2. Redix- 4 and Split Redix. Application of FFT algorithms.
Spectral analysis and convolution using FFT'

Unit §: Digital Filters and Realization

Introduction of digital filter, Basic types of filtering, Transfer function. Freguency respoase.
Adaptive filter and application. Reatization of digital filter: Direct form - L. Direct form - I
Cascade. Paraliel. Lattice and Ludder.

Unit 6 : Finite Duration Impulse Response (FIR) Digital Filters

FIR filter design by Fourier approximation, Gibbs phenomena in FIR filter Design
approximations. Applications of window functions o frequency response smoothing: Window
functions. Rectangular. Hanning. Hamming and Kaiser windows: FIR filter design by the
frequency sampling method; FIR filter design wsing the Remez exchange algorithm: Linear
phase FIR filters. Unit sample response symmetry. Group delay.

Unit 7 : Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) Digital Fitters

Classical filter design using polynomial approximations, Butterworth, Chebychev. Etliptic and
Bessel forms, IR filter design by transformation-matched Z-transform, Impulse-invariant
transform and bilirear transformation. Application of the bilinear lransformation to IIR lowpass
discrete filter design. Speciral transformation. Highpass. Bandpass and noich filters,
